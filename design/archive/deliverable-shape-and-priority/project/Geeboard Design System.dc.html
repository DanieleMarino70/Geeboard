The system behind the panel

Tokens, components and interaction rules for a dark-first game server panel. Every value below is the same variable the product reads at runtime — change it here and the panel follows.

01

Colour

Declared in HSL so hue, saturation and lightness stay independently tunable. Dark is the primary experience; the light map re-derives every token rather than inverting it. Status hues never double as the accent, so a green button can't be mistaken for a healthy state.

03

Space, radius, elevation

One 8px rhythm with two half-steps for dense controls. Radius grows with the size of the surface: 6px on a chip, 20px on a wizard shell. Elevation is used sparingly — three levels, and the third only for things that trap focus.

04

Motion

Motion exists to explain a change of state, never to decorate. Nothing animates for longer than 320 ms except a status indicator that has to keep signalling. Every rule below collapses to zero under prefers-reduced-motion.

Log viewer & timeline

10

Responsive & accessibility

Below 900px the panel is not a narrower desktop: the sidebar becomes a five-item bottom bar, cards become full-width rows, tables collapse to stacked key–value blocks, and the console gets its own full-height route.
