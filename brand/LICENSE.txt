The Geeboard name and mark
==========================

Geeboard's source code is licensed AGPL-3.0-only; the full text is in
LICENSE at the root of this repository, and that file is the licence
itself and is not modified by this one.

This file covers something the AGPL does not: the name "Geeboard" and the
mark in this directory — geeboard-mark.svg, geeboard-mark-brand.svg,
geeboard-mark-two-tone.svg, geeboard-icon.svg, apple-touch-icon.png,
og.png and the artwork they are drawn from in source/.

  The name and the mark are not covered by the AGPL grant.
  All rights to them are reserved.

What that means in practice, and it is meant to be unsurprising:

  * You may fork, change, host and redistribute the software. That right
    comes from the AGPL and nothing here narrows it.

  * You may use the name and the mark to say what your fork is built
    from — "based on Geeboard", "a fork of Geeboard", a screenshot, a
    link, an article about it. That is nominative use and it needs no
    permission.

  * You may not present a fork as Geeboard: not under this name, not
    under this mark, not with an icon a person would mistake for it.
    Rename it and draw your own. A mark exists so that somebody
    installing a panel knows whose panel it is, and a fork wearing this
    one takes that away from them.

  * If you are unsure, ask first: open an issue on
    https://github.com/DanieleMarino70/Geeboard

Removing the mark from a fork is one directory and four references; they
are listed in brand/README.md.
